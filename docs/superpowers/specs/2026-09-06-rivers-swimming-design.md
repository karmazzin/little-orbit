# Mountains, watersheds and swimming

Approved in conversation: distinct settlement valley, hills and mountain ridges; multiple connected river basins around the sphere, tributaries, descending streams, through-flow lakes and a small sea. Author the drainage network and shape the terrain around it. No fluid solver, diving, stamina or new quests.

F on desktop and a contextual touch button enter water from a nearby accessible bank, or leave it at an accessible bank. Walking never enters water; swimming never moves onto dry terrain. Falling into water automatically starts swimming. Currents gently move swimmers. Bridges and a dry stepping-stone crossing preserve existing quest access.

The same spherical terrain/water sample drives geometry, collisions, camera clearance and saves. Water elevation varies along rivers and agrees at confluences. Preserve story progress and reconcile old player positions with the changed surface. Raise clouds/atmosphere if mountains require it. Keep WebGL rendering batched and mobile controls usable.

Acceptance: connected downhill drainage on multiple sides, visible relief, functional entry/swim/exit and fall recovery, clear controls on desktop/touch, accessible existing quest points, passing tests/build and browser verification.
